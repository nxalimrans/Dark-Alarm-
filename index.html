Six ways Dark Alarm keeps its word

Every alarm gets its own wake-up lock, schedule, and sound — configured exactly how heavy a sleeper you are.

Math Challenge

Solve equations, not excuses. Pick Easy, Medium, or Hard and set how many problems stand between you and silence — from simple arithmetic to solving for x.

Scan Object

Place a real object across the room the night before. The only way to stop the alarm is to walk over and scan it — no reaching over from the pillow.

Extreme Mode

Math and Scan, combined into one challenge. Built for the truly unwakeable — sleepers who've out-smarted every other alarm on their phone.

Flexible Scheduling

Run alarms every day, on chosen weekdays only, or just once — each with its own label, challenge type, and sound.

Auto-Stop Duration

Questions, answered

Dark Alarm FAQ

What is Dark Alarm?
Dark Alarm is a dark-themed Android alarm clock app that requires you to complete a short wake-up challenge — a math problem, a real-object camera scan, or both — before it will stop ringing.
How does the Math Challenge feature work?
Turn on the Smart Wake-Up Lock, set the challenge type to Math, choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty, and pick how many problems to solve. When the alarm rings, every problem must be answered correctly before it stops.
What is Scan Object mode?
Scan Object asks you to point your phone's camera at a specific real object — like a toothbrush or a light switch — that you chose in advance. Because the object has to be somewhere other than your bed, you physically have to get up to stop the alarm.
What is Extreme Mode?
Extreme Mode requires both the Math Challenge and the Scan Object task on the same alarm. It exists for heavy sleepers who can complete a single challenge without fully waking up.
Is Dark Alarm free to download?
Yes. Dark Alarm is free to download and use, with no purchase required to access the wake-up challenge features.
What Android version does Dark Alarm require?
Dark Alarm requires Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer.
Can I set multiple alarms with different challenge types?
Yes. Each alarm is configured independently, so one alarm can use a Math Challenge while another uses Scan Object or Extreme Mode, each with its own schedule, label, and sound.
Can I set an alarm to repeat only on certain days?
Yes. Each alarm can repeat every day, on specific weekdays you choose, or ring once and turn itself off afterward.
What happens if I can't finish the wake-up challenge?
Every alarm has an Auto Stop Duration, such as 15, 30, or 60 minutes. If the challenge isn't completed within that window, the alarm stops automatically so it never rings indefinitely.
